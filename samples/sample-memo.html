Northwind Roasters — Second Location Memo

Date: July 22, 2026 · Participants: Blake Parsons (owner), Maya Chen (flagship GM), Daniel Ortiz (finance lead)

We should open the Fremont location in March — the numbers now clear every bar we set in January, and the lease window closes in six weeks.

Weekend foot traffic on the corridor is up 34% year over year, and our wholesale accounts in the neighborhood have doubled. The catering pilot we ran in the fall converted “casual” customers into subscribers at nearly twice the café rate, which tells us demand is durable rather than seasonal.

The bet is simple: our brand travels, our margins survive a second rent, and the team is ready to split.

What the numbers say

Unit economics at the flagship have held for nine consecutive months. The $41 average weekly subscriber spend is the strongest signal — it funds the buildout without touching the reserve.

  • Buildout estimate is $180k against a $210k budget. The $30k gap comes from the espresso line, the largest single line item, which we already sourced below its budgeted cost; the remainder stays in the budget as contingency.
  • Staffing plan promotes two shift leads rather than hiring managers externally.
  • Break-even lands in month seven under the conservative traffic model.

Risks we are carrying

The two we cannot hedge: a second espresso machine delay would push opening past the summer lull, and the corridor construction project, if extended, cuts our morning window for a full quarter.

Everything else is reversible. The lease has a sublet clause, the equipment holds resale value, and the staffing changes are promotions we would want to make anyway.
